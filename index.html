How much is the service call?

Service call depends on distance & time. I’ll quote before dispatch — no surprises. You save the $1k+ tow to the dealer.

Yes — Freightliner, Volvo, Kenworth, Peterbilt, International, Western Star with Cummins, Detroit, Paccar, Volvo engines. If unsure, text me your VIN.

Absolutely. I carry OEM software to perform parked regens, reset ash levels, and test aftertreatment sensors roadside.
